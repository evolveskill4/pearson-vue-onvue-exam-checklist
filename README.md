## 4-Step Pearson VUE & OnVUE Pre-Exam Checklist

1. **Government ID Name Match:** Verify that your Pearson VUE profile's first and last name match your valid government-issued photo ID character-for-character.
2. **Pre-Exam OnVUE System Diagnostics:** Run the official OnVUE system test on the exact laptop and Wi-Fi network you will use on exam day to verify webcam, microphone, and bandwidth stability.
3. **Clean & Private Testing Environment:** Clear your desk of dual monitors, notes, phones, and smartwatches, and ensure a quiet, well-lit room.
4. **30-Minute Early Check-In:** Log in to your appointment at least 30 minutes prior to your scheduled slot to complete ID photos and proctor verification smoothly.
